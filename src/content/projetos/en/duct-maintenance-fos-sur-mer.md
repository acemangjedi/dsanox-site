The work was planned day by day: the new sections arrived prefabricated and the team came on site with the sequence of cutting, assembly and welding already set.

The job was done at height, from scaffolding, and ended with the inspection of the welds before the plant started up again.

- Corrective maintenance of ducts
- New prefabricated sections
- Welding at height
- Inspection before start-up
