The route runs above existing equipment, so each line was measured on site, manufactured in the workshop and fitted in short working windows.

Assembly included the supports, the connections to the equipment and the final check of each line against the project specifications.

- Site survey
- Manufacture of piping and ductwork
- Assembly in a plant in operation
- Final check, line by line
