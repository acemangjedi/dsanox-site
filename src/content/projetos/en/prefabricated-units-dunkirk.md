Each unit left the workshop assembled on its own base, with the pipework welded, the surfaces treated and painted and the access ways fitted. On site, it only had to be positioned and connected.

Doing the work in the workshop shortened the time on site and allowed everything to be inspected before transport.

- Preparation and machining of the components
- Welding and pre-assembly
- Sandblasting and painting
- Quality control before dispatch
