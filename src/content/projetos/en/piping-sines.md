The lines were prefabricated in the workshop, in numbered spools, and taken to site with the surface protection already applied. On site, the team positioned them, secured them to the supports and made the closing welds.

Quality control followed both stages: inspection of the spools before dispatch and checking of the connections after assembly.

- Prefabrication of the spools in the workshop
- Supports and flanged connections
- Assembly and welding on site
- Inspection before and after assembly
