The structure was manufactured in the workshop to tight dimensional tolerances, so that the elements would fit on site without adjustment. Assembly was done in stages, with the factory running in the hall next door.

Lifting and positioning followed a work plan agreed with the customer, to keep the access routes clear throughout the job.

- Columns, beams and support elements
- Lifting and positioning on site
- Securing and welding
- Assembly in stages, without stopping production
